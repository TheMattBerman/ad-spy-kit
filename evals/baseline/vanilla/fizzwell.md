## **Offers and Hooks Being Used**

1. **Discounts with Deadline/Urgency:**  
   - “30% off every 12-pack, today only.”  
   - “Ends tonight: 30% off.”  
   - *Urgency* is hammered home.

2. **Evergreen (Layering Subscription):**  
   - “15% forever. Cancel anytime.”  
   - Snares subscription/LTV.

3. **Gifting (Holiday Angle):**  
   - “Gift a variety pack: 12 flavors, one box.”  
   - Smart for holiday/bulk gifting.

4. **Doorbuster, Shipping:**  
   - “Free shipping on any 2 packs.”  
   - *Lower urgency and perceived value than % off, but helps with AOV push.*

5. **Exclusive/Cyber Monday Launches:**  
   - “Early access: the Cyber Monday flavor drop.”  
   - Flavor drops and exclusivity are underused!

6. **Product Launch/Feature:**  
   - “Meet Cherry Cola.”  
   - Good for flavor fans, not a mass appeal hook.

7. **Nostalgia/Functional:**  
   - “Tastes like the soda you grew up on. 2g sugar.”  
   - *Combines emotional hook with health cue.*

## **Gaps & Weaknesses**

### **1. **Lack of true UGC or social proof**
- All hooks are direct, deal/call-to-action or feature-driven.
- *No reviews/testimonials, zero UGC screenshots, no “see what fans say.”*  
- Missed opportunity to build trust and FOMO.

### **2. **No explicit bundles or higher-order AOV offers**
- No “Stock up for the holidays” or “Family pack” or “Buy 3, get 1 free.”
- Gifting angle tapped, but bundling/cross-sell is not.

### **3. **No influencer hooks or faces**
- All copy-driven, not face/founder- or influencer-led creative (“Why I switched,” “Fizzwell taste test” etc).

### **4. **No comparison/competitor-busting**
- No “Why Fizzwell vs XYZ Soda,” or breakouts like “30% less sugar than LaCroix.”
- Could win conversion at comparison stage.

### **5. **Repetitive copy—lack of creative risk**
- Main winning ad is 30% off, but every other promo is a simple tweak on price, shipping, or early access.
- *No “twist” ads—no humor, shock, or viral stunts.*

## **What Ads You Should Run Against Them**

### **A. Out-Conversion with Social Proof**
**Hook:** “See why 10,000+ shoppers say ‘Fizzwell is the only soda I’ll buy again’”  
- Short rapid-fire UGC video montage—testimonials, star ratings overlay, unboxing.
- Frame discount/offer as “trusted by thousands” + “sale ends soon.”

### **B. High-AOV Holiday Bundle Push**
**Hook:** “The Ultimate Gift Pack—Save 40% when you get 4 or more!”  
- Promote higher bundle discount (go 35-40% if possible) for gifting/sharing.
- Bonus: “Free shipping on bundles today only.”

### **C. Comparison/Competitor Attack**
**Hook:** “Fizzwell vs LaCroix: Same fizz. 80% less sugar. 30% off, Black Friday only.”  
- Use split-screen, feature breakdown, flavor reviews.
- Highlight unique benefits: lower sugar, more flavors, satisfaction guarantee.

### **D. Founder/Influencer-Led UGC**
**Hook:** “Even my mom was skeptical. Now she fills her fridge with Fizzwell.”  
- Selfie video from founder/influencer, product in shot, authentic taste reaction.
- “Here’s why I switched,” “My honest review” angle.

### **E. Viral Angle / Meme**
**Hook:** “POV: You show up to the holiday party with Fizzwell. *You’re the hero*.”  
- Meme video/GIF, playful and relatable.  
- *Stand out by using humor or trendjacking.*

### **F. Story-Led Ad**
**Hook:** “[Name], 33: ‘Soda was my guilty treat. Now I never feel bad about it.’”  
- Mini-story, before/after problem/solution using Fizzwell.
