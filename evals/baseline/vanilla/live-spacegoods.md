## 1. **What’s Winning?**

### **Top Performing Ads**

- **Format**: UGC video is dominating. Nearly all top spenders (“bangers” and high banger_band) are authentic, talking-head, or “shows what customers say” video.
- **Core Message Formula**:  
  - “You’ve tried everything: routine, sleep, supplements. It’s not you, it’s missing this one thing: Rainbow Dust.”
  - *Sub-hook*: “You’re closer to fixing it than you think.”
- **Winning Offers**:
  - **Huge, time-limited discounts** (“up to 58% off”, “double discount”, “deal ends tonight”)
  - **Stacked offers:** “plus five free gifts,” “free express delivery,” “60-day money-back guarantee”
  - **Big risk-reversal:** “Full refund if nothing shifts”
- **Ad Proof**:
  - **Duration:** Top ad is live 78 days with 3 versions—proof it’s a solid performer.
  - **Variation:** Same message repurposed across creator channels & image ads.  
  - **Customer Results Used As Hooks:** “My husband lost a stone without changing his diet,” “@spacegoods is my cheat code for productivity”
  - **Education:** Explains how standard coffee is “breaking your brain,” or that “focus requires calm” and how adaptogens create true energy.

## 2. **Popular Offers & Hooks**

### **Offers**
- **Headline Offers:** “Up to 58% off”, “double discount (stacked with 5 free gifts)”, “free express shipping,” “60-day money-back guarantee”
- **Smaller offers:** Creator-specific codes, usually 20% off, seed belief there’s a real deal, but the mega-offer appears more attractive.

### **Hooks**
- **Problem Agitation:** “You’re not getting dumber. Your brain is depleted. Only one is fixable.”
- **Curiosity/Education:** “Why coffee is ruining your focus,” “The missing piece in your routine,” “3 reasons you can’t concentrate and none of them are your phone.”
- **Social Proof/Influencers:** “My cheat code for productivity”, testimonials and story-driven customer scripts.
- **Urgency:** “Deal ends tonight,” “Be quick.”

## 3. **Where are the Gaps? What’s Missing?**

1. **Cluttered Message — “You’ve Tried Everything”**  
   - The “You’ve tried everything...” script is used in almost EVERY ad (copy-paste across creators, images, videos), leading to creative fatigue.  
   - Over-reliance on this “broken record” format = diminishing returns & audiences tuning out.

2. **Little Creative Innovation:**  
   - Every ad either “shows what customers say,” “tells a story,” or “education/curiosity” about the product’s ingredients vs coffee.
   - No bold “Black Friday/Cyber Monday” themed packaging, countdowns, or live deal thermometers.
   - No overt **price anchoring** (how much are you saving in $/£?), no “Before/after” style results.
   - Scarce use of authority (doctors, experts), or science breakdowns beyond simple ingredient lists.

3. **Lack of Explicit Product Comparison:**  
   - They highlight “you’d spend more on separate supplements,” but there’s no direct apples-to-apples grid: “Rainbow Dust vs. Coffee vs. Leading Mushroom Brand.”
   - No consumer reviews/ratings visual, or clear value/quantity graphics.

4. **Limited Demographic Diversity:**  
   - Heavy on women around 30-40, wellness creators; may be missing men, older adults, or students/biohackers.

5. **Missing Brand-Story or Founder-Led Ads:**
   - No deep “about us,” “why we built this,” or “behind-the-scenes” for emotional connection.
   - Lacks UGC from people with different health backgrounds (parents, athletes, WFH, ADHD, menopausal, students).

6. **Neglecting User Experience / Ritual / Unboxing:**
   - They claim “delicious taste,” but there are few ads showing the first sip, unboxing experience, or making the drink on camera.

## 4. **What Ads Should YOU Run Against Them?**

### **A. Outflank Their "Tired Message"**
- **Fresh Hooks:**
  - “89% of people make these focus mistakes—here’s how to fix it this winter”
  - “How does YOUR supplement stack up? The 5 questions to ask before you buy”
  - “Why do peak performers switch from coffee to [Your Brand] this holiday?”
- **News-style BFCM Angle:**
  - “Black Friday Deal Tracking: Only 102 Rainbow Dust bundles left at 60% off (live countdown)”
  - “We made too much for Black Friday—get it for less than our own staff price”
  - “Cyber Monday Early Access: 24h Only—Everything Must Go!”
  - Use timers, overlays (countdown graphics), or dynamic deal counters.
- **Price Anchoring:**  
  - Visual grid: “How much you’d pay for each benefit (with Amazon links),” vs. your bundle.
  - Example: “Coffee = £80/mo, Supplements = £100/mo, [Brand] = £24 for all!”

### **B. True “Why Now” BFCM Scarcity**
- **IRL video of team packing orders, emphasizing “orders outpacing stock,” real “selling out” moment.**
- **Founder/CEO pop-in:** “I’m the founder. I promise—this is the only time this year you’ll get more than 50% off. Here’s why...”

### **C. Comparison & Proof Stacking**
- **Grid-style creative:**
  - “[Brand] vs Spacegoods vs Four Sigmatic vs MudWtr”
  - Benefits tickbox, price, flavor, guarantees—make “your” superiority glaring.
- **UGC/Micro-influencers** from different demos: men, athletes, students, older adults, biohackers.
- **Doctor or authority endorsement:** “I recommend this blend for real focus without the crash.”

### **D. Showcase Ritual, Taste, Results**
- **Unboxing, mixing, first-sip videos:** Highlight flavor and experience, *not* just benefits.
- **Before/after testimonials:** Real stories: “From 4pm brain fog to all-day focus”

### **E. Emotional & Holiday-Linked**
- “Give the gift of clarity. Gift bundles for BFCM—special packaging, limited supply.”
- Any creative leveraging “new year, new you,” “get ahead of your 2027 goals.”

## **TL;DR**

- **They’re very good on discount stacking, UGC, and brain-focused problem agitation, but stuck on “You’ve tried everything.”**
- **Hit them with BFCM urgency, real video proof of deals, strong product comparison (grid), more price anchoring, diverse UGC, and emotional (holiday/gifting/new year) angles.**
- **Show not just how your product works, but what makes it best—especially during this once-a-year deal window.**
