## 2. Where Are the Gaps and Weaknesses?

**A. Lack of Deep Social Proof**
- Only one ad appears to use real customer experience (ad-4). No reviews screenshots, before/afters, "over X sold!", etc.

**B. Scarcity & Sellout Messaging Not Utilized**
- No “sets are selling out”, “only X left”, “don’t miss out” urgency.
- No use of countdowns or “ends in 48 hours!”

**C. No Visual Demonstration of Product Results**
- No before/after, texture shots, or IRL results beyond ad-4’s testimonial.

**D. Limited Creativity in Offer Framing**
- Focused mostly on bundles and free gifts; missing:
    - Sitewide %
    - “Best seller” callouts
    - Gift guide/“for everyone on your list”
    - “Buy X, gift Y” offers

**E. Static Creative Skew**
- Images > video/carousel overall, limiting DPA (dynamic product ad)/retargeting flows.

**F. Weak Retargeting / Mid-Funnel Angles**
- All ads direct to collection/set pages; no cart-abandon, review-driven, “compare us” or competitor-conquest creative.

## 3. If You’re Competing: What Ads Should You Run?

### i. **Beat Their Value Stack—Up the Offer and Scarcity**
- **Example Offer:** “Black Friday: 3 full sizes for $54 ($90 value) + Free Shipping—Ends Sunday!”
    - *Even if your offer matches, amp perceived value and urgency.*

- **Hook Ideas:**  
    - “Only 500 Black Friday sets left—don’t miss out!”

- **Format:** Video + carousel (show each set piece + value math on-screen)

### ii. **Leverage Stronger Social Proof, Reviews, UGC**
- **Ad Idea:** “4.9⭐ from 2,500+ happy faces—see why everyone’s switching”
    - Collage of reviews, before/afters, UGC snippets
    - *Overlay:* “Shop Black Friday—last chance for the lowest price of the year”

- **UGC Script:**  
    - “I’ve spent $$$ on other brands—nothing saved my winter skin like this set!”  
    - Show “before” and “after” (bonus: use real customer photos with permission)

### iii. **Countdown/Scarcity and Urgency**
- **Ad Idea:**  
    - “Black Friday Sale Ends In: [Live Countdown Timer]”
    - “Only X sets left”

- **Format:** Story/vertical video, bold countdown, quick shots of people adding to cart

### iv. **Gift-Giving / Gifting Angle**
- **Ad Idea:** “Holiday gifting? The set everyone actually wants. Ships free by [date].”
    - *Add: “Gift wrap included while supplies last!”*
    - Show product being opened as a gift; “shopper testimonial” about easy gifting.

### v. **Comparison/Competitor-Conquest**
- **Ad Idea:** “Glow without the splurge—compare us to [Dewbar]/‘big brands’ and save $30+ this Black Friday”
    - Quick side-by-side feature/price bullet points
    - Reviews from customers who switched

### vi. **Abandon Cart/Retargeting**
- **Ad Idea:** “Did you forget something? Your Glow Set is still waiting—grab it before Black Friday ends!”
    - “Unlock an EXTRA free mini when you complete your order now.”

### vii. **Holiday Themed Dynamic Product Carousel**
- Match their carousel, but run DPA ads based on past browsing with overlays: “Holiday pricing live! Tap your favorite.”

## 4. Recap: Your Winning Playbook

**What’s working for them:**  
→ Simple, strong value bundle; threshold gift; urgency wording; some UGC/testimonial.

**What’s missing:**  
→ Depth of social proof, scarcity, reviews, gifting, retargeting, creative formats, and “why us” angle.

**What to run:**  
- Out-value their bundle and/or make it appear even more valuable
- Lean hard on social proof (reviews, real faces, before/afters, “thousands sold”)
- Add FOMO: countdowns, “selling fast” overlays, last chance reminders
- Giftable, holiday, and competitor-comparison angles
- Deploy DPA, video, carousel, and UGC video formats
- Don’t neglect abandoned cart/mid-funnel retargeting

---

**TL;DR:** Dewbar’s Meta ad play is strong at the top of funnel, but lacks heavier social proof, dynamic urgency, and creative gift messaging. To win: out-bundle, out-social, add real urgency, and hammer their weak spots with creative retargeting and gifting angles.
