### 2. **What Offers and Hooks Are They Using?**

- **Big Black Friday/Cyber Monday Discount:** Heavily features “up to 47% off.”
- **Product Longevity & Quality:** “The last wallet you’ll buy,” “Used for six years.”
- **Exclusivity/FOMO:** “VIP Early Access.”
- **Flash Sale Urgency:** “47% off — 48 hours only.”
- **Bundling Value:** “Bundle the carry system,” pairs wallets/rings for more cart-value.
- **Brand Scarcity:** “Why we rarely discount.” (To frame the sale as rare and worth acting.)
- **Asset Types:** Split between video (testimonials, explanation) and image (bold, simple claims), with at least one carousel for product variety.

---

### 3. **Where Are the Gaps / What’s Missing?**

- **Social Proof Is Surface-Level:**  
  Only one testimonial style ad (“I’ve carried the same wallet for six years.”) and it’s not UGC-heavy. No obvious authority or review/press features.

- **No Head-to-Head Demos:**  
  **No side-by-side comparison** to classic “fat wallets”/bifold wallets or competitors.

- **Lack of Gifting Angle:**  
  **No mention of “Perfect Gift”**, “Gift Boxes,” “For Dad/Husband/Boyfriend.” Black Friday/Cyber Monday is a huge gifting season—they’re missing this emotional/generosity lever.

- **No Mega Bundles / Upsells:**
  While “Bundle the carry system” is present, it lacks specificity (“Save $X on wallet + ring + key organizer + bottle opener”).

- **Minimal Visual Variety:**  
  Many creative derivatives but few mention/depict actual daily use, no “what fits” content, no tactile ASMR/unboxing.

- **No “Zero Regret” Safety Net:**  
  Little mention of warranty, free returns, money-back guarantee (risk reversal for high-ticket wallet buyers).

### 4. **What Ads Should You Run to Beat Them?**

#### **A. Make It Gifting Season**
- **Visual:**
  - “This Black Friday, give the only wallet he’ll ever need.”
  - Unwrap/holiday box visuals, “Gift Ready” badge.
  - “Personalize It” option for initials/engraving.
- **Copy:**
  - “The #1 Gift for Guys (That They’ll Use Every Day)”
  - “Comes in a gift box — Ready to wrap.”
  - “Free extended holiday returns.”

#### **B. Pound Social Proof/UGC**
- **Visual:**
  - Grid of real reviews: “4.9 stars from 18,000+ verified buyers.”
  - UGC video mashup: “Why Foldbrook is my all-time favorite wallet.”
- **Copy:**
  - Pull quotes: “Broke my old wallet on day 1. This is indestructible!”
  - Overlay ratings, press logos: “As featured in GQ/Wired.”

#### **C. Head-to-Head, Hands-On Demos**
- **Video:**
  - “Why I swapped my old bifold for Foldbrook.” (Show wallets side-by-side, pros/cons, EDC breakdown.)
  - “What I fit in my Foldbrook vs regular wallet.”
- **Hook:**
  - “Stop pocket bulk for good. Foldbrook = 1/3 the size.”

#### **D. Reward Urgency, Add-Ons, Risk Reversal**
- **Flash Sale:**
  - “48 hours only: Extra 15% off for early access. Ends midnight.”
  - “Free Engraving + Free Shipping over $50”
  - “100-day trial. Not obsessed? Free returns.”

#### **E. Bundle-Specific Calls**
- **Visual:**
  - “Build Your Ultimate EDC Kit: Wallet + Key Organizer + Ring = Extra 20% Off at Checkout”
  - Price breakdown, save visuals.

#### **F. Newness (Color, Drop, Custom)**
- **Launch New Colors with Scarcity:**
  - “Limited Edition colors drop Black Friday only—stock won’t last!”
